# Model on what already works

Before designing a new product feature, pricing, or flow, study successful
comparable products online (for Showlnk: Laylo, Linktree, Posh, DICE,
Feature.fm, Partiful and the like). Note what they charge and how the flow
works, model a similar version, and record the research with sources in the
plan (see `docs/plans/03-billing.md`, "What works elsewhere").
